# UpscaleAI — Enhance Beyond Limits

Premium AI image upscaling platform. Users upload images, the **real
Real-ESRGAN x4plus model** enhances them on CPU, and results are compared
with a Before/After slider and downloaded as high-resolution PNGs.

- Accounts, server-side credits, admin dashboard, and manual bKash/Nagad
  payments (admin-approved, 1 BDT = 1 credit).
- Scales 2x/4x only (CPU benchmarked); batch up to 10 + ZIP download.
- One job at a time; CPU-only, so minutes per image, not seconds.
- Upscaling generates plausible detail — it does not recover guaranteed
  original detail.

Full source, setup, and honest limits:
https://github.com/wpsabbir688/upscaleai
